How to Connect Mic & Use VoxSynth DD-6

  • 1. 3.5mm Wired Headset / AUX Line-Out (Ultra-Low Latency) Plug in a 3.5mm wired earphone, TRRS mic, or AUX cable to a mixer/PA speaker. Select 3.5mm Wired Mode, tap MIC LIVE, and raise MIC GAIN (+6dB to +18dB) and GATE until room hiss disappears.
  • 2. Bluetooth Speaker / TWS Setup (A2DP Stereo Quality) Switch AUDIO ROUTE to Bluetooth Mode. Choose "Phone Built-in Mic + BT A2DP Speaker" so Android keeps crisp stereo speaker quality while you sing into the phone mic. Keep Bluetooth Anti-Feedback ON and tune the 0–250ms Sync Slider.
  • 3. Tactile Knobs, WARP/REV & 5.2s HOLD Looper Drag vertically on any knob, scroll wheel, or double-tap to reset. Turn MODE to WARP/REV for swirling pitch-modulated reverse echoes, or HOLD to record & loop vocal phrases up to 5.2 seconds via the metallic stomp pedal.
  • 4. Backing Track Overdubbing & Export Upload an MP3/WAV/OGG/M4A backing track (or load the built-in Studio Groove), toggle TRACK FX Dry/On, press REC on the pedal to capture the combined mix, and export lossless WAV or 192kbps MP3 from the Library.
